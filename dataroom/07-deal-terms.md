# The deal

**R1,000,000 for 10% of the equity, plus the R1m returned out of R1 from every
tin sold, until repaid.**

---

## Terms

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Amount** | R1,000,000 |
| **Timing** | One payment, at closing. No second round in the plan. |
| **Equity** | 10% of the ordinary shares — permanent |
| **Repayment** | R1.00 per tin sold, from the first sale |
| **Tins to repay** | 1,000,000 — **modelled as month 50** |
| **Security** | None. Ranks behind trade finance. |
| **Dividends** | None until the R1m is repaid; thereafter 50% of profit above a R1.5m working-capital buffer |
| **Board** | No board seat at 10%. Annual shareholder update, and financials on request. |
| **Anti-dilution** | None. The investor dilutes pro rata with everyone else on any future issue. |

### If the business is sold at the end of year 5

Year-5 EBITDA is R9.49m. Equity value = enterprise value + surplus cash − trade
finance − any capital still owed.

| Multiple | Equity value | Founders (81%) | PJ (9%) | **Investor (10%)** | **Investor IRR** |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| **4x** | 44,594,487 | 36,121,534 | 4,013,504 | **4,459,449** | **49%** |
| **6x** | 63,579,887 | 51,499,709 | 5,722,190 | **6,357,989** | **58%** |
| **8x** | 82,565,288 | 66,877,883 | 7,430,876 | **8,256,529** | **65%** |

**Every multiple here is an estimate and nobody has offered anything.** It is
arithmetic on a plan whose volumes are still assumptions — a way of seeing how
value would be shared, not a valuation.

## What is not in this deal

**No second round is planned.** The business is EBITDA-positive in year 2 and
funds itself from there. If the 90-day pilot beats plan materially, a growth
round becomes an option — but it is not in the model and it is not being asked
for.

**No security and no personal guarantees.** The investment ranks behind the trade
finance facility, which is secured on stock.

**No board seat at 10%**, and no reserved matters beyond standard minority
protections. We would rather offer information and access than governance
machinery at this size.

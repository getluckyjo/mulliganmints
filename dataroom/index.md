# Mulligan Mints — investor dataroom

**Like it never happened.**

A strong pressed mint in a tin, built for golf. Proved in South Africa, then
licensed to the world.

**The ask: R1,000,000** — 10% of the equity, plus the R1m returned out of R1 from
every tin sold until repaid.

## Two things we would rather you heard from us

**The cost side is quoted; the revenue side is not.** Every rand of cost sits on
a real supplier quotation — R0.465 FOB, 25% duty, 800 kg minimum order, 60-day
production. Not one rand of revenue does. **Tins sold per outlet per month has no
published benchmark anywhere in the world**, and the whole plan scales off it.
That is what the R1m is for.

**We have written down what could go wrong.** Folder 08 contains a risk register
with eighteen scored risks and a decision log covering nine decisions, including
the ones we reversed. Most rooms do not include these. We think you should be
able to see how the business is being run, not only what it hopes to become.

**And we have modelled being wrong.** The workbook carries a downside built on
this plan's own structure, not a different one: at 55% of planned sell-through
year 5 lands at R9.70m of revenue and R2.78m of EBITDA. It still does not need a
second round — but your capital is not repaid inside five years and no dividend
is declared. That case is in folder 03, next to the plan, not buried.

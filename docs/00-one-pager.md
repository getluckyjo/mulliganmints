# Mulligan Mints — one page

**Like it never happened.**

---

**What.** A genuinely strong sugar-free pressed mint in a 35g tin worth keeping.
Irish-inflected heritage design with a modern edge. **Strong peppermint at
launch**; further flavours chosen on real sell-through.

**Why it exists.** Strong mints are an old, unbranded category owned by
multinationals who do not defend it. No mint has an occasion. This one does: the
mulligan — the shot you take again, the one that never counted.

**Who for.** Golfers, and the people who drink with them.

**Where.** Golf club pro shops and halfway houses, then bars and restaurants,
then national retail, then export — then licensed globally.

**Why South Africa first.** 460 golf clubs, 139,496 registered golfers, 4.2m
rounds a year. Small enough to prove sell-through by hand. Cheap enough to prove
it for under R1m of stock.

**Why it travels.** Golf content is now a global media business — Good Good Golf
raised USD 45m on USD 40m+ of revenue; Bob Does Sports has 1.1m subscribers and
has already launched two consumer brands. A South African mint brand can be built
on a global media channel at South African cost.

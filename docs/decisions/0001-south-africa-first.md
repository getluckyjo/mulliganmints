# 0001 — Prove the concept in South Africa first

**Date:** August 2026 · **Status:** Accepted

## Context

The eventual prize is global — licensing or export. The question was whether to
prove the concept in South Africa or to launch directly into a larger golf market
such as the US or UK.

## Options

1. **South Africa first**, then export, then licence
2. **United States first** — largest golf market, where the creator audience is
3. **UK/Ireland first** — closest to the brand's design reference

## Decision

South Africa first.

## Why

- **460 golf clubs is a list, not a market.** One person can physically visit
  every club that matters. That is not true of the US or the UK.
- **The evidence is cheap here.** The whole pilot costs about R30,000 of stock
  and 90 days.
- We are here, and the relationships are here.
- **139,496 registered golfers and 4.2m rounds a year** is enough volume for the
  sell-through data to be real rather than anecdotal.

## Consequences

- The domestic market alone will not produce a venture-scale outcome. South
  Africa is the proving ground, not the prize — the plan must always carry a
  credible global route or it is just a small importing business.
- Being a South African brand is a fact to be handled deliberately in export
  markets, not hidden. See the origin-claim constraints in
  `research/04-global-opportunity.md` §6.
- Everything in years 1–2 is optimised for producing *evidence*, not profit.

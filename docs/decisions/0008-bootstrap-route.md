# 0008 — The bootstrap route is the plan

**Date:** August 2026 · **Status:** Accepted
**Supersedes the funding approach in** [0003](0003-export-then-licence.md)

## Context

Two routes were modelled side by side:

| | Bootstrap | Funded |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Capital | **R1,000,000** | R3.5m pre-seed + R12m growth round |
| Channels | Golf, bars, DTC — all direct | + national retail + export |
| Global route | Licence only | Export first, then licence |
| Year 5 revenue | R11.92m | R43.63m |
| Year 5 EBITDA | R4.47m at **38%** | R12.59m at 29% |
| Break-even | **~2,700 tins/month** | ~15,600 tins/month |
| First EBITDA-positive | **Year 2** | Year 3 |
| Founder ownership | **80%** | ~52% |

## Decision

**The bootstrap route is the plan.** R1m, once. No growth round.

The funded plan stays modelled in `finance/5-year-plan.md` as the scale
alternative, and as the answer to "what would it take to go faster" — but it is
no longer the plan of record.

## Why

**Break-even is 2,700 tins a month, not 15,600.** That is the fact that decides
it. A business that breaks even on roughly 60 golf clubs and 40 bars does not
need R15.5m — it needs enough to buy stock and survive year one. The funded plan
was carrying a cost base the proposition does not require.

**Three advantages the funded plan never leaned on.** Two marketing executives
rather than one founder; existing access to 30 golf courses and 600 promotions a
year through Get Lucky; and branding, website and content at no cash cost. Those
make a small raise sufficient rather than merely brave.

**The margins are better, not worse.** 38% year-5 EBITDA against 29%, because
national retail costs R1.4m in listing fees and gives up R6 a tin, and export
gives up R9 a tin plus the working capital to carry stock in someone else's
market.

**Ownership.** 80% against 52%, on a business reaching R4.5m of EBITDA either way
within a factor of three.

## What we give up

**Absolute scale.** R11.92m of year-5 revenue against R43.63m. This is a good
business, not a venture outcome, and it must not be pitched as one.

**National retail.** Checkers, Clicks and Dis-Chem stay closed. Golf and bars cap
out around 300 clubs and 2,500 metro outlets.

**Speed against the category window.** A golf creator or a Mondelēz line
extension could take premium strong mints while we get to 313,000 tins.

## Consequences

- `pitch/investor-narrative.md` rewritten around the R1m ask
- Export is out of the plan entirely — decision 0003 is superseded on that point;
  the global route is **licensing only**
- Retail is out of the plan; the channel ladder ends at bars and DTC
- The funded plan and the bear/base/bull scenarios remain in the model as
  comparators, and `finance/5-year-plan.md` is relabelled accordingly

## Revisit when

- **The 90-day pilot beats plan materially.** Then the growth round becomes a
  real option, raised off evidence at a far better valuation than a pre-revenue
  round would have fetched. Nothing here forecloses it.
- **A licensee or a golf creator offers a partnership that needs scale capital.**
  That is a different conversation and worth having on its merits.

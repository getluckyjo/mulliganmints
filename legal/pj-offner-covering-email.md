Hi PJ,

Great chatting yesterday. Here's the term sheet for the brand work, as discussed —
10% of the company for the identity, packaging and guidelines.

A few things I've written into it that we should talk through:

**The shares are issued to you on day one**, so you're a real shareholder from the
start, not someone waiting on a promise. They vest in four tranches as the work
gets delivered — 20% on signature, then against the identity, the packaging
artwork and the brand book.

**Dilution.** There's a table in clause 7 showing what happens to your 10% as we
raise. After the pre-seed and the growth round you'd land around 5.8%. I'd rather
you see that now than be surprised by it in two years.

**IP.** The company needs to own the brand outright — that's the one I can't move
on. The whole plan from year four is licensing this brand into other markets, and
no licensee signs anything without clean title. You keep full credit and portfolio
rights from launch.

**Future work is paid.** This covers the brief. New flavours, campaigns, a
refresh, anything for a licensee — separate and paid, and you get first refusal
for three years.

One thing worth flagging: shares for services are taxable in your hands under
section 8C, and the tax can land before there's any cash to pay it with. Please
get your own advice on that before you sign — I'm getting mine.

Have a proper read and mark it up. Anything you want to push back on, push back.
I'd rather sort it now than paper over it.

Want to jump on a call this week?

Warm regards,

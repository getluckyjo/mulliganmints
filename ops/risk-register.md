## The four that actually matter

**R-18 (month-12 cash)** — the highest score on the register and the most
urgent. R16,432 of headroom is not a plan. Getting trade finance to month 10
instead of 13 fixes it for nothing; R1.25m of equity fixes it for 2.5% of the
company. Do the first.

**R-17 (distributor dependency)** — new with decision 0009. One partner now holds
every outlet we have. Contract the data and the performance minimums.

**R-01 (sell-through)** — the assumption everything else rests on, and the
cheapest to test. R30,000 of stock and 90 days answers it.

**R-11 (single-supplier dependency)** — still open, and more exposed now that a
single flavour from a single factory is the entire launch. Get the second
supplier quoted on the real spec.

**R-05 (creator competition)** — the one we cannot control, and the one that
argues most strongly for moving quickly and for treating US golf creators as
partners rather than rivals.

*R-02 (supplier price) has dropped off this list — the quote arrived and it is
now a known number rather than a guess.*

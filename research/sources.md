## Known gaps

These numbers do not exist publicly and are **estimates** in our model. Listed
here so nobody mistakes them for research:

1. **SA mint category size and brand shares.** Our R1.2–1.5bn estimate is
   derived. Fixable by buying the Euromonitor *Sugar Confectionery in South
   Africa* report.
2. **Count of licensed on-consumption outlets in SA.** No consolidated national
   figure exists; licensing is provincial. Stats SA's 5,178-enterprise frame is
   a weak proxy.
3. **Golf-course retail sell-through.** No benchmark exists anywhere. Must be
   measured in our own pilot.
3b. **What share of SA golf clubs lease their pro shop rather than running it.**
   We have three worked examples, not a survey — and it decides who we call.
   Answerable by telephone. See `gtm/sales-and-distribution-strategy.md`.
3c. **Whether any distributor treats golf clubs as a defined channel**, and
   whether golf club groups buy centrally. We found no evidence either way.
4. ~~Supplier FOB cost per tin.~~ **Answered** by the Suntak quotation above:
   USD 0.465 FOB Shantou. What remains estimated is the *volume curve* — every
   price tier above 150,000 units a year is extrapolated from that single
   observation and should be quoted directly.
5. ~~Suntak's minimum order quantity.~~ **Answered:** 800 kg of candy per
   recipe = 22,944 tins per flavour. One thing still to confirm in writing —
   that it is per flavour rather than a total across the order.
6. **SA confectionery trade margins by channel.** Only Indian FMCG comparators
   were findable (distributor 10–20%, retailer 20–30%). Our channel margins are
   estimates built from RSP working backwards.
